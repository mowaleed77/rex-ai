# FAQ

**Q: Can I pay in installments?**
A: Installment plans are available for courses above 4,000 EGP. Contact admissions to arrange one.

**Q: Do I get a certificate?**
A: Yes, an academy completion certificate is issued after passing the final assessment. It is not an official accreditation unless stated on the course.

**Q: Are sessions recorded?**
A: Online sessions are recorded and available for 30 days.
